Quick guideHow the quote works+

Use slicer material including supports, rafts and purge. Print labor is hands-on time per run, separate from machine print time. Additional labor applies once per job.

The batch multiplier repeats material, print time and print labor. For different runs, add their estimates together and use a batch multiplier of 1.

Target margin is profit after labor, costs and selling fees, divided by the customer total. It is not a markup. Prices round up to the next cent.

For resin bottles labeled in grams, convert to ml using the resin density. Film lifespan is not required. Add a known film allowance to Extras if needed.

Customer totals include shipping and exclude sales tax. Owner labor + profit is an allowance before income tax. Quotes and rates are stored in this browser separately for Resin, FDM and UV printing.

UV ink usage

For advance quotes, choose a similar completed print and adjust relative ink use to allow for size, coverage, white and gloss. The examples come from your print history. Their dimensions and layer settings are unknown, so they are comparison references, not calibrated coverage presets. A visual indicator alone cannot be converted reliably to ml. Enter your own measured reference for a closer match.

Known total and per-color entry remain available for measured usage. Rounded channel amounts may differ from the reported total. The selected method alone is charged; the material buffer applies afterward. Print time is entered separately and is not used to predict ink.

Blank / substrate unit cost

Use the quantity of usable pieces from the pack or sheet. The quote uses this unit cost multiplied by the blank quantity for the whole job. Leave either job amount at zero to omit it.
